# Expense and Travel Policy

Guidelines for submitting business expenses and booking travel at Bearn Analytics.

## Submitting expenses

All business expenses must be submitted through **Ramp** within **30 days** of the purchase date, with an itemized receipt attached. Expenses submitted after 30 days require manager approval to be reimbursed.

## Approval thresholds

- Expenses **under $200**: auto-approved against your manager's monthly expense budget.
- Expenses **$200–$2,000**: require direct manager approval.
- Expenses **over $2,000**: require both manager and department head approval.

## Travel booking

Flights and hotels should be booked through **Navan**, the company's designated travel platform. Domestic flights should be booked in economy class; flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Hotel spend is capped at **$250/night** in standard cost-of-living cities and **$400/night** in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo).

## Meals and per diem

When traveling for business, meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to a **$75/day** cap, or employees may opt for a flat **$60/day per diem** with no receipts required.

## Client entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable at up to **$150 per person** with a brief note on attendees and business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable as part of a client meal but not as a standalone expense.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Bearn Analytics does not reimburse: personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, airline lounge memberships, or upgrades to first class on flights under 6 hours.
