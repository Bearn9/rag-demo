# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

This document describes Bearn Analytics' expectations for where and when employees work.

## Hybrid schedule

Employees based within **50 miles** of a Bearn office are expected to work in-office on **Tuesdays and Thursdays**, with the remaining days flexible between home and office. Employees living beyond that radius are considered fully remote and are not required to come into an office.

## Core hours

Regardless of work location, all employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration during **core hours of 10:00 AM–3:00 PM Eastern Time**. Outside of core hours, employees may structure their day flexibly as long as deliverables and team commitments are met.

## Home office stipend

Fully remote employees, and hybrid employees on their remote days, are eligible for a **$500/year home-office stipend**, reimbursable for desks, chairs, monitors, and other ergonomic equipment. Stipend requests go through the standard expense reimbursement system.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to **30 consecutive days per year** without special approval, provided they remain in a similar time zone (within 3 hours of their home office) during core hours. Longer stays require HR and Legal sign-off due to tax and employment-law implications.

## Office access

All office locations use badge access via the employee's Okta-linked ID badge, available 24/7 for employees who need after-hours access.
