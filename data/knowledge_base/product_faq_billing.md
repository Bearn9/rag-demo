# Product FAQ: Pricing and Billing

Answers to common questions about Bearn Analytics platform pricing and billing.

## Plan pricing

Bearn Analytics is offered in three tiers, billed monthly or annually:

- **Starter:** $49/month. For individuals and small teams getting started with analytics dashboards.
- **Team:** $99/month. Adds collaboration features, shared dashboards, and additional data connectors.
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing. Includes SSO/SAML, dedicated support, and advanced governance controls. Contact sales for a quote.

## Annual discount

Customers who choose annual billing receive a **20% discount** compared to paying month-to-month on the same tier.

## Refunds and cancellations

New subscribers are covered by a **14-day refund policy**: if you cancel within 14 days of your first payment, you receive a full refund, no questions asked. After the 14-day window, cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, with no partial refunds for unused time.

## Payment methods

We accept all major credit cards and, for Enterprise customers, invoicing with net-30 payment terms. Failed payments trigger three retry attempts over 7 days before an account is downgraded to a read-only state.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Plan changes take effect immediately for upgrades (prorated charge for the remainder of the billing cycle) and at the next billing cycle for downgrades.
