# Health Benefits, 401(k), and Parental Leave

This document summarizes the core benefits available to full-time Bearn Analytics employees.

## Health insurance plan tiers

Bearn offers three medical plan tiers through our benefits provider, selectable during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event:

- **Bronze:** Lowest premium, higher deductible. Company covers 70% of premium.
- **Silver:** Balanced premium/deductible. Company covers 80% of premium.
- **Gold:** Highest premium, lowest deductible. Company covers 90% of premium.

Dental and vision coverage are bundled at no additional cost on all three tiers.

## 401(k) retirement plan

Employees are eligible to enroll in the 401(k) plan on their first day of employment. Bearn matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary**, with immediate vesting on the match. Contributions can be traditional (pre-tax) or Roth, or a combination of both.

## Parental leave

Bearn Analytics provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** for all new parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement), regardless of gender or which parent is the primary caregiver. Leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the qualifying event, subject to manager approval for the split arrangement.

## Other benefits

- Annual wellness stipend of $300, reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health apps.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering free confidential counseling sessions.
- Life and short/long-term disability insurance provided at no cost to the employee.
