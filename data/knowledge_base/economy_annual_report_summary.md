# Annual Report Summary: FY2024

A high-level summary of Bearn Analytics' financial year 2024 (Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2024) performance, for internal reference. Full quarterly figures are tracked in `economy_quarterly_financials.csv`.

## Fiscal year and reporting

Bearn Analytics' fiscal year runs **January 1 through December 31**, matching the calendar year. Financial results are reviewed by the board quarterly and summarized in an internal all-hands deck published the second week after quarter close.

## FY2024 highlights

Full-year revenue for FY2024 reached **$5.86 million**, up **34% year-over-year** from FY2023's $4.41 million. Full-year net profit was **$1.23 million**, giving a blended profit margin of roughly 21%. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the year at **$6.15 million**, driven primarily by Team and Enterprise tier upgrades.

## Funding history

Bearn Analytics raised a **$2.5 million seed round** in early 2021, led by **Cascade Ventures**, with participation from **Birchline Capital**. The company has been cash-flow positive since Q3 2023 and has not raised additional external funding since the seed round — growth since then has been funded from operating cash flow.

## Cash position and runway

As of Q4 2024, cash reserves stood at **$4.6 million**, giving the company a runway well in excess of 24 months at current burn (the company was net cash-flow positive for all of FY2024, so "runway" is a formality rather than a constraint).

## Budget approval process

Departmental budgets are proposed by department heads in November and approved by the CFO and CEO in December for the following fiscal year. Any single unbudgeted expense over **$10,000** requires CFO sign-off regardless of which department's budget it falls under.
