# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

This document describes how paid time off accrues and how to request it at Bearn Analytics.

## Accrual schedule

Full-time employees accrue PTO on a tiered schedule based on tenure:

- **Year 1–3:** 15 days per year, accrued monthly (1.25 days/month).
- **After 3 years:** 20 days per year, accrued monthly (1.67 days/month).
- **After 7 years:** 25 days per year, accrued monthly.

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to scheduled hours.

## Requesting time off

All PTO requests must be submitted through **Workday** at least **5 business days** in advance of the first day off, except in the case of documented emergencies. Requests are approved by your direct manager and are subject to team coverage needs during that period.

Requests longer than 10 consecutive business days require additional sign-off from your department head.

## Rollover and payout

Up to **5 unused PTO days** may be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any unused balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st, except where state law requires payout.

Upon separation from the company, unused accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's current base rate, in accordance with local law.

## Holidays

In addition to PTO, Bearn Analytics observes 10 paid company holidays per year, published annually on the internal HR calendar.
